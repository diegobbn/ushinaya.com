Concrete Love — Collective Exhibition

Explanation


Concrete Love is a collective exhibition developed by twenty-two students from the CEPV (Centre d'enseignement professionnel de Vevey) made for the BIENNALE IMAGE VEVEY 2026. Displayed from August 31 to September 4, 2026.
At a time when digital communication saturates our screens with continuous streams, how can we give substance back to ideas, commitments, and shared imaginaries? Created by 22 students from the CEPV, Concrete Love explores this question through posters and slogans. The project takes the form of a choral work inspired by the exquisite corpse game and is designed using silkscreen printing. Through this collective adventure, the project celebrates the power of connections woven through experimentation, the circulation of voices, and working together.

The images presented here are all works from the exhibition in which my contribution appears.
