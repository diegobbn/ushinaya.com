CAPTCHA

SHORT FILM · 2026 · CEPV × VIFFF · VEVEY

Prove you're human.

CAPTCHA is a short film developed as part of a collaboration between the CEPV (Centre d'enseignement professionnel de Vevey) and the Vevey International Funny Film Festival (VIFFF) in 2026.

Over the course of eight sessions spread across several weeks, students were invited to discover filmmaking and create short films that would eventually be presented during the festival. Working in groups of four, we approached the project as an opportunity to explore a medium that was largely unfamiliar to us.

Without any previous formal training in cinema, this project represented our first real introduction to filmmaking. From developing an idea to shooting and post-production, we discovered the different stages involved in bringing a film to life. Throughout the process, we were accompanied by external professionals who shared their experience and helped us explore different aspects of film production, including cinematography, sound and colour grading.

Three short films were created by the different student groups and presented at the 2026 edition of VIFFF in Vevey. CAPTCHA is the result of this collective experience: a first encounter with filmmaking, built through experimentation, collaboration and learning by doing.



THE FILM
A job interview takes an unexpected turn as the identity of the recruiter becomes increasingly uncertain. As the conversation unfolds, the boundaries between human and artificial intelligence begin to blur.

Through an unsettling encounter, CAPTCHA explores the relationship between human identity, technology and the growing ambiguity of our interactions with artificial intelligence.

FESTIVAL SCREENING — VIFFF 2026

Following its production at the CEPV, CAPTCHA was presented during the 2026 edition of the Vevey International Funny Film Festival.

The screening brought together the three short films created by the student groups as part of the collaboration between the school and the festival.

This public presentation marked the conclusion of the project and offered us the opportunity to share our first filmmaking experience with an audience in a cinema.
